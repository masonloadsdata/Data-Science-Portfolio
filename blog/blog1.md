# Blog 1 of many to come!

Modeling social topics and people matters because your product must stand out to investors and be personalized to consumers. As technology changes our very way of life, the little things become more and more important. People you know is one of the strongest things you can take advantage of in this new era. Knowing people and convincing them to help, invest, or mentor you can be beneficial whether it be your product, skills, or even life. Making something different is something everyone is looking for.
You see this in many places, your car, office, phone, house, even house. Personalization grants people the privilege of being unique in this new barren technological world. Culture still matters. Individuality still matters. What we want to look like matters more now than ever. 

It is important to use data to accomplish relevant data outputs, and how we use it is even more important. Ethics often become overlooked and the average person is a defenseless target to the questionable, maybe unethical, methods of big data. When you go to a new website or sign an online document, there is a long page of "Accept Terms and Conditions" or "Accept Privacy Policies" which the average person is not going to look through. As humans, we often take the easy route of getting what we what and companies take advantage of this to get our data. 

Many times, people like me know what data is but not where it can take you. Learning skills to navigate data can get you far and adding context can take you to where you need to go. Data is meant to be taken advantage of and used to better our lives, but not at the cost of people's privacy.

Through this course, I want to better understand what data is, how to use it in context, and create something that impacts the people of the world.
